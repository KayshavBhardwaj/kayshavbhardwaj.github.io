LLMs Leak Secrets Through Unrelated Outputs
NeurIPS 2026 Workshop: Beyond Private Training, The New Landscape of AI Privacy (InfPriv)
Kayshav Bhardwaj (first author). ACTION NSF AI Institute, UC Santa Barbara
LLMs can statistically leak secrets through unrelated outputs without reproducing them: a white-box likelihood attack identifies a hidden PIN from one strictly filtered number list and recovers 48% of held-out PINs from repeated outputs.
