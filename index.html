Research fellow, June 2026 to present

NSF ACTION AI Institute

UC Santa Barbara SecLab, with Dr. Yigitcan Kaya

Can an LLM leak a secret without ever saying it? In my first-author paper, accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 InfPriv workshop, a white-box likelihood attack identifies a hidden PIN from one strictly filtered list of numbers, and recovers 48% of held-out PINs from repeated outputs. I'm continuing this work on token entanglement and subliminal learning toward a follow-up paper.

Student researcher, June 2023 to present

Berkeley AI Research

Dharmamitra, with Prof. Kurt Keutzer

Machine learning that translates underserved Asian languages into English. I built MITRA-Swarm, a multi-agent architecture that injects explicit morphophonemic and discourse structure into foundation models for classical Sanskrit translation. It beats compute-matched self-refinement baselines under an independent two-judge evaluation, and my lead-author paper was accepted at the NeurIPS 2026 LP4FM workshop.

Read more about BAIR

AI4Musicians Lab

Purdue University

Automatic music transcription: turning recordings into the notes that were played. I lead a research team of more than 50 members, organized the IEEE-sponsored 2025 AMT Challenge, and co-authored its results paper at the NeurIPS 2025 AI for Music workshop, benchmarking transcription models from Google, Spotify and ByteDance.

GPS is the most monitored signal in the world, and one of the most complex systems the Space Force runs. I trained a machine learning model that detects signal-to-noise anomalies in time-series GNSS data, for use by the U.S. Space Force.
